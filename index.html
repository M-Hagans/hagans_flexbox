Reflection: The hardest thing that i had to look up was the centered hero. I wasn't sure how this was supposed to be formatted based on the structure of this page. Once I saw an example of how the text was supposed to be centered in the section I started to get the idea. I have seen nav bars on websites where the links were at the top of the page. I have seen centered hero sections on hompages where there are titles and messages in the middle of the screen. i have also seen responsive card layouts on sites where products are shown in seperate boxes. The cards move around depending on the size of the screen. I feel like I am starting to get an understanding of Flexbox and how it can be used to make different layouts.
